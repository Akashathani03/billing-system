# Frequently Asked Questions

## Is my shop's data separate from other shops?

Yes. This is a multi-shop billing system — every shop's customers,
products, and bills are completely isolated from every other shop. A shop
can never see or affect another shop's data.

## How do I create a bill?

Open "New Bill", choose a customer, add one or more products with a
quantity, then tap "Generate Bill" to finalize it (or "Save Draft" to
finish it later). See billing-help.md for the full walkthrough.

## How do I add a customer?

Go to the Customers page and tap "Add Customer", then enter a name and
mobile number (address is optional).

## How do I add a product?

Go to the Products page and tap "Add Product", then enter a name and
price (unit is optional).

## How do I mark a bill as paid?

Open the pending bill and tap "Mark as Paid". This only works one way,
from pending to paid, and only on a finalized bill.

## What does pending payment mean?

It means that bill's full amount hasn't been collected yet, even though
the sale itself is complete and finalized.

## How can I view my bills?

The "Bills" page lists finalized bills under Paid/Pending tabs with
search; the "Drafts" page lists bills not yet finalized.

## What information is shown on an invoice?

Shop details, customer name and mobile, the date, each item with quantity
and price, the subtotal and total, the total in words, and the payment
method and status. See invoice-guide.md for the full list.

## How does the shop/multi-shop system work?

Each shop has its own account and its own completely separate set of
customers, products, and bills. There is no shared or "marketplace" data
between shops — everything one shop sees belongs only to that shop.

## Can I edit a bill after it's finalized?

No. Once a bill is finalized with "Generate Bill", its items and prices
are locked. The only thing that can still change is its payment status,
from pending to paid.

## Does this system charge GST or tax?

No. This billing system does not calculate or include any GST or tax — a
bill's total is simply the sum of its item totals.
