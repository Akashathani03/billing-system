# Payments Guide

## Payment methods

A bill records one payment method: cash, UPI, card, or credit.

## Paid vs. pending

Every bill has a payment status of either "paid" or "pending" for its
entire amount — there is no partial-payment tracking; a bill is not paid
"halfway", only fully paid or fully pending.

## What "pending payment" means

"Pending" means the full amount of that bill has not been collected yet.
It is still a completed, finalized sale — it just hasn't been paid for.
Credit sales are commonly left as pending until the customer settles up.

## Marking a bill as paid

Open a pending bill and tap "Mark as Paid". This can only move a bill from
pending to paid, never the other way around, and only for finalized bills
— a bill that's already marked paid cannot be marked paid again, and a
draft has no payment status to change yet.

## Payment totals collected

The dashboard shows how much money has been collected, broken down by
payment method (cash, UPI, card, credit), for a given period.

## Outstanding amount

The "outstanding amount" is the total value of all pending (unpaid)
finalized bills. It only ever includes finalized bills — a draft was never
a completed sale, so it's never counted as outstanding.

## Customers who owe money

A customer "owes money" when they have one or more finalized bills still
marked pending. The amount they owe is the sum of those pending bills'
totals. A customer whose bills are all fully paid does not appear on an
"outstanding customers" list.
